# Survey results

## Agreeing with a benefit and a concern at once

If a% agreed with one statement and b% with another, at least a + b − 100% of respondents must have agreed with both. The largest overlaps between a benefit and a concern:

| Benefit | Concern | Agreed with both, at least |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Q10 | Q9 | 50% |
| Q1 | Q9 | 46% |

Every one of the 40 benefit–concern pairs has an overlap of at least 20%.
